## Two things to raise with Prof. Ahmad Sobri

**1. The FYP's own review does not follow this rule.** `Interim-report/Docs/02-content.md` §2 is
headed *"20 papers, 8 domains, **2016–2025**"* and includes Settles & Meeder (2016), Clement et al.
(2019), and Qin et al. (2021). So either the 4-year rule applies only to the *main body* of sources
with foundational exceptions permitted, or it was relaxed for the FYP. **Confirm which** before
applying it strictly here — the answer changes whether Mayer, Banda and any other theory anchor can
stay.

**2. A review this recent has a failure mode of its own.** 13 of 19 sources are from 2026, and
several are arXiv preprints with no citation history (see `_Index.md` verification column). A
reviewer may read that as chasing novelty rather than engaging with an established base — the
mirror image of the problem the rule exists to prevent. The theory anchors (Mayer 2024, Ningrum
2026) and the peer-reviewed venues (PROBE at ACL, ChemersiveLLM at VRST, LearnMate² at DIS, Shofa in
*Physics Education*) are what keep it grounded. **Do not cut those to make room for more preprints.**

## Standing rule

Nothing here is cited until it has been opened and its bibliographic record confirmed at source —
per `IEEEPaperOutlineandReferenceGuide.md` §4 and the verification column in `_Index.md`. Roughly
half these entries still need author lists or venue confirmation.
