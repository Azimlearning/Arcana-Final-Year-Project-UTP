# PRD — Target Users and Personas (stable)

| Attribute | Aisha — Undergraduate | Daniel — Postgraduate | Mei — Exam-Prep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context | Final-year undergrad writing her FYP literature review | Master's student managing a large, growing thesis corpus | Undergrad revising dense modules before exams |
| Primary goal | Understand how 15–20 papers relate/agree/conflict; draft a review | Maintain a connected KB; track gaps over months | Convert notes/readings into durable knowledge |
| Frustrations | Tools answer per-document; connects ideas manually | Re-uploading, re-reading, losing context | Summaries help reading, not remembering |
| Key Arcana value | Cross-document synthesis, contradiction reports, grounded drafts | Persistent graph, gap analysis, incremental updates | Flashcards + spaced repetition, quizzes, Socratic tutoring |
| Main modes | Research, Writing | Research, Exploration | Study, Socratic |

**Secondary persona — Dr. Rao (Supervising Academic):** may review a student's KB. Arcana should be credible to this reader (clear citations, transparent sourcing); supervisor-specific features (oversight dashboards, cohort management) are out of scope.

**Jobs to be done:** connect a stack of related documents to understand the topic as a whole; get grounded, cited draft material while writing; turn revision material into active-recall practice; preserve knowledge base and context across sessions.
