# PRD — Problem and Background (stable)

**Background:** NotebookLM normalised "chat with your sources" — upload, ask, get grounded answers. Arcana accepts that baseline and targets the structural limitations that remain once it's met.

**Problems Arcana addresses:**
- **P1 — Flat, siloed knowledge.** Vector chunks per document; no cross-source connections/contradictions visible to the retriever; global questions unanswerable.
- **P2 — Non-composable agents.** Agents run in isolation, can't delegate; multi-step tasks need manual decomposition by the user.
- **P3 — Static, one-size-fits-all interfaces.** Same layout regardless of task (comparing papers vs. drafting vs. flashcards).
- **P4 — Research tools ignore learning science.** Retrieval/summarisation without evidence-based study support.

**Target outcome:** move from a pile of unconnected documents to genuine understanding — cross-source insight, grounded written work, retained knowledge — in a tool that reshapes around the current task.
